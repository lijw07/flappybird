Sprite preparation done for this project: individual bird colour strips were taken from `AllBird1.png`; `pipe_top.png` / `pipe_bottom.png` were assembled from the green column of `SimpleStyle1.png` by tiling the seamless body row and attaching the cap; `ground.png` is the grass ground tile from the same sheet; `medal_platinum.png` is a recoloured copy of Kenney's gold star.

Sound mapping: `flap.ogg` = phaserUp2, `point.ogg` = powerUp2, `hit.ogg` = lowRandom, `die.ogg` = lowDown.
